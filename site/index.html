Costs and rates

Every rate is a base rate times how far its index has moved since that rate was set. When Stats SA or the fuel price moves, every rate and every BoQ priced from them moves with it. A scheduled task adds the new index values on the 8th and 28th of each month. Replace market rates with your own supplier quotes as you get them.

Scanner

Every hour at seven minutes past, the scanner reads every open advert on the National Treasury eTenders portal (national and provincial departments, all municipalities, SOEs such as Eskom, Transnet and SANRAL), stores new ones, notices closing-date changes and addenda, and sorts each advert by CIDB grading. Adverts that do not state a grading but look like civil or mechanical works are checked against their documents by the Tender Desk three times a day, and you get an email summary.

What counts as a match

CIDB classes

PE (potentially emerging) designations such as 5CE PE are included. Quotations and panels count as matches. Saving re-sorts adverts that Tender Desk has not yet checked.
